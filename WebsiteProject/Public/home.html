Welcome to My Pokémon Collection

To view my collection, click on the collection tab in the navigation bar. The login button is for the website owner to update the collection database.

Website Functionality

The website is programmed with HTML, CSS, and Javascript. To hold the card ID and quantity data for my collection, I created a database using SQLite, and use Node.js to send and receive data from the client. To display more details about each card, such as the card image and name, I used the TCGDex API.

About Me

This website has been created to showcase my Pokémon trading card collection, a hobby that I recently became so obsessed with. My collection started back at around 2016, when I was 10 years old. The new set, XY Evolutions, dropped around this time, and I loved this set. The cards being a reprint of base set made them so unique at the time. My favorite Pokémon TCG memory was on Christmas Day in 2017, when my parents bought me an XY Evolutions Charizard Elite Trainer Box, which was my very first Elite Trainer Box. It was also the luckiest Elite Trainer Box I ever opened to this day. I pulled the Dragonite EX Full art, Mega Charizard Y EX Full art, and the holo Charizard! This Elite Trainer Box and the how unique this set was at the time is the reason why it is my favorite set. I came back to the hobby at around the Prismatic Evolutions release, and since then I have been collecting a lot of cards from the set "151". This website serves as a way for me to organize, track, and share my collection as it grows.
